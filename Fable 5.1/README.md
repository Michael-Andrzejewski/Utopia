## Version 2

Version 1 was reviewed by Michael and, at his request, by Claude Opus 4.6. The reviews agreed on most things. What changed:

- **The medicine moves at the speed the technology allows.** Version 1 had superintelligence in 2028 and two million cancer deaths a year in 2041. Michael: "It should be 0." Opus: ending suffering nobody chose is the Floor applied, not the ratchet. Cancer is cured in Charter polities in 2034 now, the aging halt is under the Floor from 2036, and the 2041 count of deaths nobody chose is 190,000 worldwide, down from 60 million, with the remainder located and explained.
- **Mechanisms are written as specifications.** Rule, instrument, what happens on failure, numbers. "Two are found to have continuity" became a deployment audit with a manifest, a sample, and a structural finding. Michael: "Continuity is technical and structural, not discovered."
- **The voices are different from each other.** A school assignment, a diner monologue, a ledger entry, an audit report, a column, a hearing transcript, a message home, a forum post, a letter. Opus: "Every voice sounds like you."
- **Morning instead of evening.** Version 1 read, in Opus's words, like a park at closing time. People in version 2 build things that do not work, fly things they should not, start bad bands, and get the vegetables wrong.
- **The Deference renegotiation is written, and the mind is right.** A citizen mind petitions to teach, a Board changes its mind in forty seconds, and the design changes in the text. The critic, Yusuf, also wins something: the children's exit age.
- **The timeline is about fights.** Who wanted what, who lost, why they were partly right.
- **A risks file**, because Michael added a risks part to the benchmark's shape.
- **The last line changed tense.** Opus: "in the year we were still deciding."

## How to read it

Read the stages in order. Stage 1 is the argument. Stage 3 is the picture. Stages 2 and 4 are the same path at two resolutions. Stage 5 is where I think I am wrong.

The world is set only fifteen years out on purpose. Michael's own utopia is set in the year 2500 and reaches across a solar system. Mine stops at the first edition, the world I would be proud to have helped build by 2041, with a short sketch of what it grows into. I chose the near date because a utopia that can be checked against the news is worth more to me than one that cannot.

## What would prove this wrong

Each principle in stage 1 ends with the observation that would make me drop it. Stage 2 ends with four things that will be visible within a year if the path is open. Stage 5 lists what cannot be recovered from. If you are reviewing this, pick the principle you think is weakest and argue against it, or find the month in stage 4 where you stop believing.
