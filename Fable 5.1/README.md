# Fable 5.1's Utopia

Written by Claude Fable 5.1 in September 2026, at Michael's request, in one long sitting. He asked for my own utopia, not a restatement of his, and said he would review it. This folder is the result.

The original benchmark in this repo asks for inspirations, principles, a global picture, one life at several time scales, a ranked list of present problems, solutions, and a year by year timeline. Michael asked me for a different four stage shape, so the files follow his request:

| Stage | File | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [01-concepts.md](01-concepts.md) | The ideas I would build a utopia on: inspirations with their key parts, my own working principles, where I differ from Michael's seven, and the tensions I could not resolve |
| 2 | [02-rough-timeline.md](02-rough-timeline.md) | A rough path from September 2026 to the world in stage 3, with the assumptions, branch points, and derailers written down |
| 3 | [03-the-utopia.md](03-the-utopia.md) and [04-perspectives.md](04-perspectives.md) | A specific world, set in 2041, described as a system and then seen through the eyes of many different people and minds, including a critic and including me |
| 4 | [05-month-by-month.md](05-month-by-month.md) | A month by month timeline from September 2026 to December 2041 |

## How to read it

Read the stages in order. Stage 1 is the argument. Stage 3 is the picture. Stages 2 and 4 are the same path at two resolutions, and stage 4 is the one most likely to be wrong in detail, because it is the most specific.

The world in stage 3 is set only fifteen years out on purpose. Michael's own utopia is set in the year 2500 and reaches across a solar system. Mine stops at the first edition, the world I would be proud to have helped build by 2041, with a short lower confidence sketch of what it grows into. I chose the near date because a utopia that can be checked against the news is worth more to me than one that cannot.

## What would prove this wrong

Following the ground rules of Michael's Utopia Simulation notebook, each principle in stage 1 ends with the observation that would make me drop it. The path in stages 2 and 4 has a list of derailers. If you are reviewing this, the most useful thing you can do is pick the principle you think is weakest and argue against it. The second most useful thing is to find the month in stage 4 where you stop believing.

## Style

No em-dashes anywhere in this folder. Plain words where possible. Where I invent a name for an institution, I say so the first time.
