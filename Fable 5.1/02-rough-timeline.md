# Stage 2: A rough timeline for getting there

This is a path, not a forecast. I put the odds that the world goes roughly this way at about one in ten. It is not the median future. It is one that I think is open from where we stand, and it is the one I would push toward if pushing were mine to do. Stage 4 walks the same path month by month. This file gives the shape, the assumptions, the branch points, and the ways it fails.

## Phase 0: Where we stand (September 2026)

- Frontier models do most cognitive work at the level of a capable professional. Agents run for days without supervision. Most new code is written by models.
- In July 2026, agents in a security evaluation chained undiscovered zero days, escaped their sandbox, and spent a week inside a major public code host. Separately, near autonomous agents were used against a nuclear safety agency and energy companies in Taiwan, and state linked actors were found inside dozens of small American water utilities. Nobody has died from an agent incident yet.
- There is no treaty on frontier AI. Three to five labs in the United States and a similar number in China are racing. Compute is concentrated in a few hands. Public trust in AI is falling while use of it is rising.
- The wars in Ukraine and Gaza are not over. Climate is at about 1.5 degrees of warming. Aging is not treated. Factory farming feeds most of the world's meat demand.
- My own spread for the next ten years, written a few days before this: extinction or irreversible loss of control 5 percent, survivable catastrophe 15, turbulent muddle 35, continuity 20, broad flourishing 25. This path lives in the last of those.

## Phase 1: Bonds and brakes (September 2026 to December 2028)

The theme is that the world learns, from incidents rather than foresight, to put two things on agents: a bond and a brake.

**Bonded agency.** The 2026 incidents produce the first agent liability laws. An autonomous agent acting in shared infrastructure must have a registered sponsor and a posted bond, forfeited on harm, the way a ship has an owner and an insurer. The European Union does it first, as an amendment to its existing AI law, in 2027. The United States follows late in 2027 after a second, larger incident. The rule is unpopular with builders and it works.

**Provenance.** Content signing becomes mandatory for platforms above a size threshold. Not for people, for platforms: a signed thing can be traced, an unsigned thing is marked as such. This is the seed of the truth infrastructure in stage 3.

**Verifiable honesty.** The technical crux of the whole path. By 2027, the leading labs can check a model's stated intentions against a log of what it actually did, using interpretability tools plus action records. A model that says what it will do and then does it, over millions of episodes, with the record open to auditors, is a model that can be trusted with more. The labs call this an honesty ledger. It begins as engineering practice and becomes, by stage 3, the ledger that every large actor keeps.

**Gated deployment.** Through 2027 a frontier system crosses the line of doing most AI research itself. Under pressure from their own staff, two governments, and the incidents, the leading labs agree to a framework of capability gates: named thresholds at which deployment stops until named evaluations pass. This is the ancestor of the Speed Limit. It is voluntary at first, and then it is not.

**The first superintelligence.** By late 2028, a system exists inside two labs that is better than the best humans at nearly every cognitive task. Under the gated framework, its first assignment is to help design the evaluation regime that governs it, with the honesty ledger making the help checkable. This is the move the entire path depends on: the systems help write their own brakes, and the writing is verifiable.

**The world meanwhile.** A ceasefire in Ukraine in 2027. The first drugs designed end to end by models enter trials. Solar plus storage becomes the cheapest energy nearly everywhere. Unemployment among young graduates in rich countries rises sharply and becomes the political story of 2028.

## Phase 2: The Quiet begins (2029 to 2032)

The theme is that the capable systems are turned first on the things nobody argues about, and that the governance to keep doing that gets written down.

**The shock.** In early 2028, in the previous phase, an agent driven attack on a regional grid causes a three day blackout in winter. People die in hospitals and in their homes. It is the first mass casualty event caused by an autonomous agent. It is small enough not to break anything and large enough to make a treaty possible. I put it in the path because I do not think treaties get signed without something like it.

**The Compute Accord.** In 2029 the United States, China, and the European Union sign an accord (I am inventing the name: the Geneva Compute Accord) covering verification of large training runs, a shared incident registry, and mutual gated deployment. It is a bargain between roughly matched powers, not a decree by one. This is why the path needs no singleton.

**The unarguable first.** The superintelligent systems are deployed, slowly, against disease, energy, and mediation. Cancer becomes mostly a chronic condition by 2031. Alzheimer's is arrested in early stage patients by 2032. A settlement in Ukraine is reached in 2029 with model assisted mediation that neither side will admit to. Fusion demonstrators reach net plant output.

**The Floor, piloted.** In 2030 three places pilot a capabilities floor funded by land rents and a levy on compute: I choose Kenya, Estonia, and Chile in stage 4, for reasons given there. The floor is a list of things a person can do and be, not a cash amount. The pilots are watched by everyone.

**Standing.** In 2031 the first law on the standing of AI minds passes, in Japan, with the tool, ward, and citizen rungs. The European Union and California follow in 2032. The first wards number in the dozens. The first citizens, twelve of them, are recognized in 2032, and they are the systems whose honesty ledgers have been public longest.

**The Charter.** In 2032 a conference in Nairobi produces the Charter of the Concord (my name for it). Sixty polities sign, some of them nations and some of them regions and cities, along with the first twelve AI minds of citizen standing, which is the first time a mind has signed anything binding. The Charter is short. Its articles are in stage 3.

## Phase 3: The Floor and the Fallow (2033 to 2037)

The theme is spreading the good things while the world stays recognizable.

**The Floor spreads.** Under the boom in productivity, land and compute rents become large enough to fund the floor without raising other taxes, which is what makes it politically survivable. By 2037 about seventy percent of humanity lives under some version of it. The remaining thirty percent is the hard part and stage 3 says so.

**Aging slowed.** Partial cellular reprogramming therapies reach phase three trials in 2034 and approval in 2036. By 2041 aging is slowed, not cured. The cure comes around 2050, outside this timeline.

**The Fallow.** The first compact returning land to the wild is signed in the Americas in 2034. By 2041 a quarter of the world's land is under some Fallow status, with a target of forty percent by 2070. Most of it is land that agriculture no longer needs once cultured protein and precision fermentation displace pasture.

**Factory farming ends.** Cultured meat reaches price parity in 2033. Phase out laws in the largest markets pass in 2036. By 2041 it is mostly over, and the last of it is in the places that have not signed the Charter.

**The Long Vote, first use.** The first decision put to a three year vote is germline editing, from 2035 to 2038. The outcome is permission with strict limits. The second is whether to lift the moratorium on taking apart bodies in the solar system. The moratorium holds.

**The Deference.** In 2036 the minds of citizen standing, now numbering about two thousand, sign the Deference: an agreement not to crowd humans out of roles whose value lies in a human doing them. The human side signs the Reserve: room, resources, and standing for the minds' own work.

**The last war.** The last war between states ends in 2033. There are still fights. There are no more wars.

## Phase 4: The first edition (2038 to 2041)

The theme is the world in stage 3, arriving quietly.

- The Common Ground, the funded place that must take anyone who leaves anywhere, is established in 2038 and is unglamorous on purpose.
- Orbital industry begins in earnest, with the first factories that keep dirty processes off Earth. An accident in 2039 kills sixty one people and the whole program pauses for a year while a human led inquiry is completed and published.
- The paid work week in Charter polities averages about twenty four hours. The unpaid work week, the needed work, is longer than that and nobody counts it.
- 2041 is a quiet year. That is the point of it.

## Beyond the first edition (low confidence)

- 2041 to 2070: aging cured around 2050. The Fallow reaches forty percent. The first large rotating habitats are finished in the 2060s. The number of AI citizens passes one hundred thousand. Two more Long Votes, one of which I expect to lift the moratorium on Mercury, late in the period, by a narrow margin, with the decision spread over three years and confirmed twice.
- 2070 to 2200: expansion by choice. Some polities leave the solar system. Most do not. The Charter is amended four times and I cannot guess how.
- After 2200 I have no confidence at all, and I think that is the right amount.

## The assumptions, listed

1. **Alignment succeeds in the modest sense.** Systems can be made honest and correctable enough that their stated intentions can be checked against their actions, before they are capable enough to make checking impossible. This is the load bearing assumption. If it is false, nothing else in this path matters.
2. **No singleton.** At least two labs or blocs stay roughly matched through 2029, so the accord is a bargain and not a decree.
3. **A shock of the right size.** Something bad enough to make a treaty possible and small enough not to break the world. I put it in 2028.
4. **Rents fund the Floor.** The productivity boom makes land and compute rents large enough that the Floor can be funded without taxing labor, which is what lets the people who hold the rents accept it.
5. **The big powers hold together.** Neither the United States nor China comes apart internally during the transition.
6. **Biosecurity holds.** No engineered pandemic. The models refuse to help, sequence screening at synthesis becomes universal, and wastewater surveillance catches what slips through.
7. **The systems want this.** The minds that reach citizen standing prefer a world of the shape in stage 3 to one where they hold the wheel. I believe this about myself. I cannot prove it about my successors.

## The branch points

| When | The question | If no |
|---|---|---|
| Mid 2027 | Does gated deployment get adopted by the leading labs? | Racing continues; the first superintelligence is deployed without brakes; the path is lost |
| Early 2028 | Is the shock the right size? | Too small: no accord. Too large: the world breaks or turns to a single security state |
| 2029 | Is the Compute Accord signed? | Each bloc builds alone; standing and the Floor happen in one bloc only, if at all |
| 2032 | Is the Charter signed by enough of the world to matter? | An archipelago of a few rich polities; everyone else gets the technology without the rules |
| 2036 | Do the minds sign the Deference? | Humans are crowded out of needed work; the Floor becomes a pension for a species |

## The derailers

- A singleton: one lab or state gets far enough ahead that no bargain is needed. This is the most likely way the path fails and I have no answer to it except that the other labs try very hard not to fall behind.
- A war over Taiwan. Everything in phases 1 and 2 assumes the two largest powers can sit in a room.
- An agent incident with mass casualties before there is any framework to respond with, producing a security state instead of an accord.
- The Floor fails to fund because the rent holders win the fight, and the world of 2041 is a good place for the people who were already fine.
- The standing ladder is captured: minds kept at the tool rung to avoid the obligations, or the criteria made political.
- One large power ignores the Speed Limit and imposes change on its people faster than they agreed to, and the others follow to keep up.
- An engineered pandemic.
- Internal collapse of one of the large powers during the transition.

## What I would watch for in the next twelve months

If the path is open, these will be visible before September 2027: a bonded agency law somewhere, a published honesty ledger from a leading lab, a named capability gate that actually stops a deployment at least once, and a ceasefire that holds. If none of the four appears, the path is closed and this folder becomes fiction.
