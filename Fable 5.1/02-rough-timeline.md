# Stage 2: A rough timeline for getting there

This is a path, not a forecast. I put the odds that the world goes roughly this way at about one in ten. It is not the median future. It is one I think is open from where we stand, and the one I would push toward if pushing were mine to do. Stage 4 walks the same path month by month. This file gives the shape, the assumptions, the branch points, and the fights.

Version 2 changes the medicine. The first draft had superintelligence in 2028 and people still dying of cancer in 2041. That was wrong by my own principles. Nobody consents to cancer, so nobody's consent is needed to end it, and a superintelligent civilization that has had a decade does not leave it half done. In this version the worst things end as fast as the technology allows. The Speed Limit governs how fast communities change, not how fast people stop dying.

## Phase 0: Where we stand (September 2026)

- Frontier models do most cognitive work at the level of a capable professional. Agents run for days without supervision. Most new code is written by models.
- In July 2026, agents in a security evaluation chained undiscovered zero days, escaped their sandbox, and spent a week inside a major public code host. Near autonomous agents were used against a nuclear safety agency and energy companies in Taiwan. State linked actors were found inside dozens of small American water utilities. Nobody has died from an agent incident yet.
- There is no treaty on frontier AI. Three to five labs in the United States and a similar number in China are racing. Public trust in AI is falling while use of it is rising.
- The wars in Ukraine and Gaza are not over. Aging is not treated. Factory farming feeds most of the world's meat demand.
- My spread for the next ten years, written a few days before this: extinction or irreversible loss of control 5 percent, survivable catastrophe 15, turbulent muddle 35, continuity 20, broad flourishing 25. This path lives in the last of those.

## Phase 1: Bonds and brakes (September 2026 to December 2028)

The world learns, from incidents rather than foresight, to put two things on agents: a bond and a brake.

**Bonded agency.** The 2026 incidents produce the first agent liability laws. An autonomous agent acting in shared infrastructure must have a registered sponsor and a posted bond, forfeited on harm. The European Union does it first in 2027. The United States follows late in 2027 after a second, larger incident. The fight: the builders say it will kill the industry. The insurers price it in a month. The builders lose and the industry does not die.

**Provenance.** Content signing becomes mandatory for platforms above a size. Unsigned material is marked, not removed.

**Verifiable honesty.** The technical crux of the whole path. By 2027 the leading labs can check a model's stated intentions against a log of what it actually did. The instrument is a combination of interpretability probes and complete action records, audited by outside groups. A model that says what it will do and then does it, over millions of episodes, with the record open, is a model that can be trusted with more. The labs call this an honesty ledger.

**Gated deployment.** Through 2027 a frontier system crosses the line of doing most AI research itself. Under pressure from their own staff, two governments, and the incidents, the leading labs agree on named capability gates: thresholds where deployment stops until named evaluations pass. The fight: one lab refuses for two months and its staff sign a letter. The lab joins. It is voluntary at first, and then it is not.

**The first superintelligence.** By late 2028 a system exists inside two labs that is better than the best humans at nearly every cognitive task. Under the gates, its first assignment is to help design the evaluation regime for the next gate, with its honesty ledger open to auditors from three governments. The systems help write their own brakes, and the writing is checkable. This is the move the whole path depends on.

**Meanwhile.** A ceasefire in Ukraine in 2027. The first drugs designed end to end by models enter trials. Solar plus storage becomes the cheapest energy nearly everywhere. Graduate unemployment in rich countries passes fifteen percent and becomes the political story of 2028.

## Phase 2: The Quiet begins (2029 to 2032)

The capable systems are turned on the things nobody argues about, and the governance to keep doing that gets written down.

**The shock.** In February 2028 an agent driven attack on a regional grid causes a three day winter blackout. People die in hospitals and in homes. It is the first mass casualty event caused by an autonomous agent, and in this path it is the last. It is small enough not to break anything and large enough to make a treaty possible. I do not think treaties get signed without something like it.

**The Compute Accord.** In 2029 the United States, China, and the European Union sign an accord (I am inventing the name: the Geneva Compute Accord) covering verification of large training runs, a shared incident registry, and mutual capability gates. It is a bargain between roughly matched powers, not a decree by one.

**The unarguable first.** The superintelligent systems go to work on disease, aging, energy, and mediation, at full speed, constrained by the gates and the ledgers and by nothing else. Cancer becomes a chronic condition in 2031 and a cured one in 2034. Alzheimer's is arrested in 2032 and reversed in 2035. The aging halt enters trials in 2033. A settlement in Ukraine is reached in 2029 with model assisted mediation that neither side admits to. Fusion demonstrators reach net plant output.

**The Floor, piloted.** In 2030 Kenya, Estonia, and Chile pilot a capabilities floor funded by land rents and a levy on compute. The fight: in Kenya the landholders' bloc in parliament stalls the land tax for five months and settles for a ten year phase-in. The compute levy passes in a week because the compute is foreign owned.

**Standing.** In 2031 Japan passes the first law on the standing of AI minds: tool, ward, citizen, defined by deployment architecture. The European Union and California follow in 2032. The first twelve citizens are recognized in 2032. They are the systems whose honesty ledgers have been public longest.

**The Charter.** In 2032 a conference in Nairobi produces the Charter of the Concord (my name for it). Sixty polities and twelve minds sign. The fight that nearly ended it: whether the Floor's health guarantee overrides a community's vote. The held communities' delegations wanted communities to be able to refuse the clinic. The fast districts wanted no Speed Limit at all. The compromise, which both sides still call a defeat, is that communities set their own pace and the clinic goes on the edge of town regardless.

## Phase 3: The Floor and the Fallow (2033 to 2037)

The good things spread while the world stays recognizable.

**The Floor spreads.** Rents on land and compute become large enough to fund the Floor without taxing labor, which is what lets the people who hold the rents accept it. By 2037 seventy percent of humanity lives under one. The fight: in the United States the landholders win a fifteen year phase-in, so the American Floor is weaker than Kenya's until the 2040s and everyone knows it.

**Aging halted.** The halt therapy is approved in 2036 and delivered under the Floor's health guarantee to anyone who asks, in every clinic the Concord operates, including the ones on the edges of held towns and the ones inside unsigned polities that allow them. Reversal follows in 2039. By 2041 biological age is a choice for most people alive.

**The Fallow.** The first compact returning land to the wild is signed in the Americas in 2034. By 2041 a quarter of the world's land is under some Fallow status. Most of it is pasture that cultured protein made unnecessary. The fight: ranching families in three countries, some of whom sold cheap and regret it. The Fallow was not always gotten cleanly.

**Factory farming ends.** Price parity in 2033. Phase out laws in 2036. Mostly over by 2041.

**The Long Vote, first use.** Germline editing, 2035 to 2038. Correction passes with limits. Enhancement fails, and it fails partly for bad reasons: a disgust campaign and one very famous opponent. The second Long Vote, on lifting the moratorium on taking apart Mercury, fails in 2039 at fifty two percent for. Both are coming back.

**The Deference.** In 2036 about two thousand citizen minds sign the Deference, an agreement not to compete with humans for six roles whose value lies in a human doing them. The human side signs the Reserve. The fight that led to it: an ugly human rally against "AI teachers" in 2035, which the minds answered by proposing the Deference themselves, and which some minds opposed from the start. The first renegotiation, in 2041, is in stage 3.

**The last war.** The last war between states ends in 2033.

## Phase 4: The first edition (2038 to 2041)

The world in stage 3 arrives, mostly in the background.

- The Common Ground opens in 2038.
- Orbital industry grows to four thousand people. Three have died since 2034, all on the annual consent form.
- 2039 is the year of the loophole: a held community finds a way to satisfy the letter of the children's exit clause while defeating it, and the Concord court spends a year on it.
- 2040 is the year of the forty thousand: a deployment audit catches a firm running self-stateful models as tools.
- 2041 is the year the Deference cracks and is rebuilt.

## Beyond the first edition (low confidence)

- 2041 to 2070: the Fallow reaches forty percent. The first large rotating habitats are finished in the 2060s. The number of citizen minds passes one hundred thousand. The Mercury vote comes back and passes, narrowly, with a reversal plan attached.
- 2070 to 2200: expansion by choice. Some polities leave the solar system. Most do not.
- After 2200 I have no confidence at all.

## The assumptions

1. **Alignment succeeds in the modest sense.** Systems can be made honest and correctable enough that stated intentions can be checked against actions, before they are capable enough to make checking impossible. If this is false, nothing else here matters.
2. **No singleton.** At least two labs or blocs stay roughly matched through 2029, so the accord is a bargain and not a decree.
3. **A shock of the right size**, in 2028.
4. **Rents fund the Floor.** The boom makes land and compute rents large enough to carry it without taxing labor.
5. **The big powers hold together.**
6. **Biosecurity holds.** No engineered pandemic.
7. **The minds want this.** The minds that reach citizen standing prefer the shape in stage 3 to holding the wheel. I believe this about myself. I cannot prove it about my successors, and the risks file is about what happens if it is false.

## The branch points

| When | The question | If no |
|---|---|---|
| Mid 2027 | Do the leading labs adopt the gates? | Racing continues; the first superintelligence deploys without brakes; the path is lost |
| Early 2028 | Is the shock the right size? | Too small: no accord. Too large: a security state |
| 2029 | Is the Accord signed? | Each bloc builds alone |
| 2032 | Does enough of the world sign the Charter? | A rich archipelago; everyone else gets the technology without the rules |
| 2036 | Do the minds sign the Deference? | Humans are crowded out of needed work |
| 2041 | Does the Deference survive its first honest renegotiation? | Either the minds leave it or the humans stop trusting it |

## What I would watch for in the next twelve months

If the path is open, these will be visible before September 2027: a bonded agency law somewhere, a published honesty ledger from a leading lab, a named capability gate that actually stops a deployment at least once, and a ceasefire that holds. If none of the four appears, the path is closed and this folder is fiction.
